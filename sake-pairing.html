Why Add Sake Pairing?

Our omakase is crafted for balance.
Sake pairing brings depth.
The right acidity sharpens sweetness.
The right aroma softens richness.
The right temperature reveals texture.
Without pairing, you taste the dish.
With pairing, you experience the dialogue.
Sake pairing is designed to enhance — never to overpower.

A quiet dialogue between sake and season

In the tradition of Japanese omakase, each sake is served in rhythm with the course— never in excess, never in haste. Subtle shifts in aroma, texture, and temperature mirror the progression of the menu, allowing sake and cuisine to unfold as one.
