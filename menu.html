What is Seasonal Omakase at Sushi Oribe?

Our omakase changes with the seasons, featuring ingredients at their absolute peak. Each course is thoughtfully composed by the chef, reflecting the character of the moment. No two visits are ever the same — this is the beauty of dining in season.
